Unless an item's `item.json` says otherwise, every item in `bundles/` and `presets/` is shared under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 4.0 International licence (CC BY 4.0)](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/):
you may use, change and share it, also in your own edits, as long as you credit its creator (the `creator` field).

By sending an item, its creator confirms they made it, or have the right to share everything in it, and agree to
share it under these terms.

The `scripts/` folder is part of AbsentMotion and follows the licence of the AbsentMotion project (GPL-3.0). Custom
tools in `tools/` will state their own licence in their `tool.json`.
